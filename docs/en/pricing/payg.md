# Pay-As-You-Go Credits

Credits are the currency for AI-powered operations on the **Pro (Managed)** plan. Each operation consumes a fixed number of credits.

## Credit costs per operation

| Operation | Credits per use |
|-----------|----------------|
| Test generation | 50 |
| Test execution | 10 |
| QA loop iteration | 30 |
| Auto-fix attempt | 100 |
| Visual regression | 15 |
| QA monitor session | 200 |
| CI scan | 200 |

## Credit packs

Purchase credits in packs. Larger packs offer a lower per-credit rate.

| Pack | Credits | Price | Per credit |
|------|---------|-------|------------|
| Starter | 1,000 | $10 | $0.01 |
| Growth | 10,000 | $80 | $0.008 |
| Scale | 100,000 | $600 | $0.006 |

## How credits work

1. **Purchase** a credit pack from the billing dashboard.
2. **Use credits** automatically as you run operations.
3. **Monitor** your balance in real time from the dashboard.
4. **Top up** anytime — unused credits never expire.

## FAQ

**Do credits expire?**
No. Purchased credits remain in your account until used.

**What happens if I run out of credits?**
Operations that require credits will be paused. Purchase a new pack to resume.

**Can I switch between plans?**
Yes. You can upgrade from Free to Pro BYO or Pro Managed at any time. Credits purchased on Managed are retained if you switch plans.

**Are there overages?**
No surprise bills. When your credit balance reaches zero, credit-consuming operations pause until you purchase more.
