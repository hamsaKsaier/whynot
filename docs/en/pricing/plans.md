## Plan details

### Free — $0/month

The Free plan is ideal for individual developers who want to get to know WhyNot. You get access to basic test generation features and can evaluate the platform with no financial commitment.

**Included:**

- 500 credits per month
- Basic test generation
- Up to 100 test executions per month
- Community support
- 1 user
- 7-day data retention

**Limitations:**

- No QA loops
- No auto-fix
- No visual regression
- No QA monitor
- No CI scan
- No team features

### Pro BYO (Bring Your Own) — $29/month

The Pro BYO plan is aimed at professional developers and small teams who want to use their own infrastructure. You get full access to all features while keeping control over your infrastructure.

**Included:**

- 2,000 credits per month
- Advanced test generation
- Unlimited test executions
- Unlimited QA loops
- Auto-fix functionality
- Visual regression testing
- Up to 5 QA monitors
- Up to 5 CI scans
- Email support (response within 48 hours)
- Up to 10 team members
- 30-day data retention

**Note:** With this plan, you provide your own infrastructure (BYO = Bring Your Own). Setup requires technical know-how.

### Pro Managed — $49/month

The Pro Managed plan delivers the full WhyNot experience with managed infrastructure. You focus on your software development — we handle the infrastructure.

**Included:**

- 5,000 credits per month
- Advanced test generation
- Unlimited test executions
- Unlimited QA loops
- Auto-fix functionality
- Visual regression testing
- Unlimited QA monitors
- Unlimited CI scans
- Priority support (response within 24 hours)
- Unlimited team members
- 90-day data retention
- Fully managed infrastructure

## Choosing the right plan

### Are you a solo developer or just evaluating WhyNot?

Pick the **Free plan**. It gives you enough scope to learn the platform and run basic test generation. You can upgrade at any time.

### Are you a small team with technical expertise?

Pick **Pro BYO**. If you already have your own infrastructure and prefer to use it, this is the most cost-efficient option. You save $20/month over the Managed plan and keep full control.

### Do you want to focus on development?

Pick **Pro Managed**. If infrastructure management isn't a core competency — or you'd simply rather save time — we handle it for you. This plan also offers the most generous limits and the best support.

### Decision guide

| Criterion | Free | Pro BYO | Pro Managed |
|-----------|------|---------|-------------|
| Budget | No budget | Moderate spend | Higher spend |
| Team size | 1 person | 2–10 people | 10+ people |
| Technical expertise | Basic | Advanced | Any |
| Infrastructure | Not required | Already yours | Not required |
| Support needs | Low | Medium | High |

## Billing and payment

### Billing cycle

- **Monthly:** You're billed on the same calendar day each month.
- **Annual:** You pay once for 12 months up front and save 20%.

### Annual discount

With annual billing you get a 20% discount on the monthly price:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Annual (total) | Savings |
|------|---------|---------------------|----------------|---------|
| Pro BYO | $29/month | $23.20/month | $278.40/year | $69.60/year |
| Pro Managed | $49/month | $39.20/month | $470.40/year | $117.60/year |

### Payment methods

- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Direct debit (SEPA, for customers in the Euro area)

### Plan changes

- **Upgrade:** The new plan takes effect immediately. The difference is prorated.
- **Downgrade:** The current plan stays active until the end of the billing period. The new plan applies from the next billing period.
- **Cancel:** You can cancel at any time. Access continues until the end of the paid period.

### Invoices

All invoices are sent automatically by email and are available at any time in your account under **Settings > Billing**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I switch plans at any time?**
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply from the next billing period.

**Is there a minimum contract term?**
No. With monthly billing you can cancel at any time. Annual billing commits for 12 months.

**What happens if I run out of credits?**
You can buy additional credit packs. See the [pay-as-you-go documentation](payg.md) for details.

**Is the Free plan time-limited?**
No. The Free plan is free forever. Use it as long as you like.

**Do unused credits carry over?**
No. Included credits expire at the end of each billing period. Separately purchased credit packs follow their own expiration rules — see the [pay-as-you-go documentation](payg.md).
