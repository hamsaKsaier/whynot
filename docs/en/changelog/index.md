# Changelog

## 2026-04-24 — Introducing Recon: pentest every release

Recon is now available. Recon runs an authorized, autonomous security scan against your app and reports only proven exploits — every finding ships with a reproducible proof-of-concept you can hand to engineering or security.

We built Recon because most scanners flood you with unverified signals. Recon inverts the workflow: the scan crawls the target, runs active probes, and confirms every candidate vulnerability before it appears in the report. No exploit, no report. That means less noise, faster triage, and findings your team can act on the same day.

Recon is rolling out behind a flag to existing workspaces this week. When it's enabled for you, you'll see a one-time in-app notice and the Recon entry in your sidebar.

[Try it now →](/recon)

See the [Recon documentation](../recon/index.md) for authorization rules, severity rubric, and quotas.
